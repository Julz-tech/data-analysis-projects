## 4. Mobile App Market Analysis
### Objective
Analyzed Google Play Store and Apple App Store datasets to identify app categories and characteristics associated with high user engagement.

### Tools
- Python
- pandas
- numpy
- Jupyter Notebook

### Skills Demonstrated
- Data cleaning
- Duplicate detection and removal
- Frequency analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Cross-platform market analysis
- Business insight generation

### Key Insights
- Games dominate the Apple App Store ecosystem
- Google Play shows a more balanced distribution of practical and entertainment apps
- Navigation and social networking apps showed high engagement levels
- Free apps with strong engagement profiles present strong advertising revenue potential

[View Project](./projects/04_app_analysis)
