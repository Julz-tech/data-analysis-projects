# Sales Analysis Project

## Objective
Analyze sales data to identify trends, revenue drivers, and opportunities for business growth.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle Sales Dataset
- Size: ~50,000 records
- Key columns: Date, Product, Region, Sales, Revenue

## Tools & Libraries
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- R (tidyverse, ggplot2)

## Steps Taken
1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, removed duplicates, standardized column names.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** 
   - Analyzed sales trends over time
   - Identified top-performing products and regions
3. **Visualization:** 
   - Plotted revenue trends by region and product
   - Created heatmaps and bar charts for top-selling products
4. **Insights & Recommendations:**
   - Q4 consistently drives the highest revenue
   - Certain regions are underperforming despite high customer traffic
   - Recommended marketing campaigns in high-conversion areas

## Key Takeaways
- Learned how to translate raw sales data into actionable business insights.
- Strengthened cross-language workflow with Python and R.
